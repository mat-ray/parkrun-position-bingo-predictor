Position Bingo Predictor

Find the finish times to aim for to tick off your bingo numbers.

How this works

Position Bingo is about the finishing position you get, not your time. Position 3, 103, 203… all tick 03; 100, 200… tick 00. The card runs 00–99.

Your position depends on how fast everyone else runs that day. This app learns from your event's past results to tell you roughly what time tends to land you in the positions that would tick the numbers you still need.

Reading the target times

For each finishing position you'll tick, we show three things, all drawn from the times that historically produced that position:

  • aim — the median time: half the time that position was reached by this time or faster. This is your target.
  • range (e.g. 24:54–26:20) — the middle half of outcomes (25th–75th percentile): run in this band and you're in the typical zone for that place.
  • full — the fastest and slowest times ever seen at that position (the extremes).

Why it's chances, not certainty

A target time doesn't pin you to one exact place — on any given week you'll finish somewhere within a spread of ~20–30 positions around it, depending on who turns up. So aiming concentrates your odds on the places you want; it can't guarantee them.

The bands above are percentiles, not guesses: the median (aim) and the 25th–75th range come straight from your event's real past results at each position. Because deeper positions are always at least as slow as shallower ones, we also smooth the curve so a later place never shows a faster target than an earlier one.

How much does aiming help?

Compared with just turning up and running any time, aiming at a recommended target meaningfully lifts your weekly chance of ticking a number — and it helps most when you need it most:

  • Down to your last few numbers, aiming can be several times more likely to tick one than running blind.
  • When you still need lots of numbers, just turning up already ticks something most weeks, so the extra lift is smaller.

The percentage shown in your results is an estimate for the numbers you currently have left, based on your event's history.

Seasons & weather

Fields swing with the time of year and the forecast. The app weights toward events around your chosen date, and — for the coming Saturday — eases targets if rain or strong wind is forecast, since a thinner field (especially at the back) means a given place is a little slower. Each event is calibrated to its own history, so weather-proof events barely move.

Predictions are guidance from past data. Actual results depend on who shows up on the day.
